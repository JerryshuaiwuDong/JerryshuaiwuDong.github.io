👋 I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Wenzhou-Kean University. I have been guided by Prof. Jiachen Li at Georgia Tech, and at Wenzhou-Kean University by Prof. Zhiqiang Gao and Prof. Zhiwu Xie at HKUST-GZ.

My research focuses on reliable AI systems that can understand multimodal information, adapt to people, and act in real-world environments. Currently, I am exploring trustworthy multimodal AI, vision-language-action models, edge AI, personalized embodied agents, and the engineering practices that make these systems useful beyond a paper.

RadTRACE clinical induction → evidence probe → safety / utility

Trustworthy Multimodal AI · First Author

RadTRACE: Diagnosing Evidence-Chain Bottlenecks in Medical Vision-Language Models under Image-Text Hybrid Attacks

Shuaiwu Dong, collaborators

IEEE 9th International Conference on Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence (PRAI), 2026 · Accepted

RadTRACE treats medical VLM safety as a diagnostic problem, separating clinical-induction auditing, evidence probing, and safety-utility routing to identify where evidence chains fail under clean, text, image, and hybrid inputs.

Drive My Way user context + history → personalized VLA → closed-loop action

Personalized VLA · Autonomous Driving

Drive My Way: Preference Alignment of Vision-Language-Action Model for Personalized Driving

Collaborative research project; see the paper for the authoritative author order.

This work studies how longer-term user context and immediate language instructions can influence a vision-language-action policy so personalization changes closed-loop driving behavior rather than only generated descriptions.
