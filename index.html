👋 I am a Computer Science undergraduate at Wenzhou-Kean University and a Research Assistant at the International Frontier Interdisciplinary Research Institute (IFIRI), where I work on medical image analysis. I also bring industry experience from Spartina Technology (米草科技), where I worked on a PCA-based cybersecurity project. That experience shaped my interest in the gap between algorithms and deployed systems—and in building AI that is not only capable in controlled settings, but also reliable, efficient, and useful under real-world constraints.

My research has been guided by Prof. Jiachen Li, formerly at the University of California, Riverside and now at Georgia Tech; Prof. Zhiqiang Gao at Wenzhou-Kean University; and Dr. Zhiwu Xie at HKUST(GZ).

My research focuses on reliable AI systems that can understand multimodal information, adapt to people, and act in real-world environments. I am currently exploring trustworthy multimodal AI, vision-language-action models, edge AI, personalized embodied agents, and the engineering practices that make these systems useful beyond a paper.
