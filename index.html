👋 I am a Computer Science graduate of Wenzhou-Kean University and a Research Assistant at the International Frontier Interdisciplinary Research Institute (IFIRI), where I work on medical image analysis. Beyond research, I am a Technical Partner at Spartina Technology (米草科技), where I work on translating AI capabilities into real-world B2B products and client-facing solutions, from multimodal and vision systems to AI automation and overseas business applications.

My research has been guided by Prof. Jiachen Li at the University of California, Riverside, and by Prof. Zhiqiang Gao and Dr. Zhiwu Xie at Wenzhou-Kean University.

I am interested in building AI systems that do more than understand the world—they should be able to reason about it and act within it. My research focuses on multimodal embodied intelligence, especially vision-language-action models and the reliability challenges that arise when deploying them in real-world environments. Ultimately, I hope to build embodied agents that can adapt to individual people and become useful collaborators in everyday life.

📝 Selected Publications

Trustworthy Multimodal AI · First Author

RadTRACE: Diagnosing Evidence-Chain Bottlenecks in Medical Vision-Language Models under Image-Text Hybrid Attacks

Shuaiwu Dong, Zhiqiang Gao*, Kuan Huang, Yue Zhao, Belal Abuhaija, Meng Xu

IEEE 9th International Conference on Pattern Recognition and Artificial Intelligence (PRAI), 2026 · Accepted

RadTRACE treats medical VLM safety as a diagnostic problem, separating clinical-induction auditing, evidence probing, and safety-utility routing to identify where evidence chains fail under clean, text, image, and hybrid inputs.

Personalized VLA · Autonomous Driving

Drive My Way: Preference Alignment of Vision-Language-Action Model for Personalized Driving

Zehao Wang, Huaide Jiang, Shuaiwu Dong, Yuping Wang, Hang Qiu, Jiachen Li

This work studies how longer-term user context and immediate language instructions can influence a vision-language-action policy so personalization changes closed-loop driving behavior rather than only generated descriptions.
