About Me

I am interested in reliable AI systems that can move from understanding multimodal information to making decisions in real-world settings, where evidence, user preferences, device constraints, and safety all matter.

My research path has moved from medical image adaptation to medical vision-language model safety, personalized driving with vision-language-action models, and edge or embodied systems that must operate under practical deployment constraints.

Research Interests

Multimodal AI· Vision-Language-Action Models· Edge AI· Embodied AI

I study how multimodal models can become more reliable, personalized, resource-aware, and effective when they are connected to real decisions and actions.
