👋 I am a Research Assistant at the International Frontier Interdisciplinary Research Institute (IFIRI), Wenzhou-Kean University, where I also received my B.S. in Computer Science.

I am interested in building AI systems that do more than understand the world—they should be able to reason about it and act within it. My research focuses on multimodal embodied intelligence, especially vision-language-action models for driving and UAV navigation, and the reliability challenges that arise when deploying them in real-world environments. Ultimately, I hope to build embodied agents that can adapt to individual people and become useful collaborators in everyday life.

My research has been guided by Prof. Jiachen Li at the Trustworthy Autonomous Systems Laboratory (TASL) at the University of California, Riverside, and by Prof. Zhiqiang Gao and Dr. Zhiwu Xie at Wenzhou-Kean University.

Beyond research, I am a Technical Partner at Spartina Technology (米草科技), where I work on translating AI capabilities into real-world B2B products and client-facing solutions, from multimodal and vision systems to AI automation and overseas business applications.

🔬 Ongoing Research

UAV Vision-Language-Action Navigation

Building on AeroDPO / AeroVLA, I study why UAV navigation VLAs fail in closed loop—flying to the wrong building, losing the target, skipping instruction sub-goals, or colliding near the goal. After reproducing the official checkpoints in TravelUAV simulation and analyzing failure modes, I am developing a training-free, test-time correction framework that keeps the base policy frozen and intervenes only when needed, guided by instruction semantics, altitude strategy, and task memory.

Ongoing Simulation-based; failure analysis done, method and multi-landmark instruction split in progress.

Frontier Models as Embodied Policies — “ASTRA as Policy” for Drones

Recent robotics work uses a general frontier model (ASTRA) directly as the robot policy. I am asking whether this transfers to aerial navigation and, more importantly, which step of the pipeline the large model actually contributes to—by comparing it against cheaper LLMs and traditional planners on the same benchmark, and by testing a hybrid setup where a small model executes and the large model is called only for high-level re-planning when the drone deviates from the goal.

Early exploration Early stage, simulation only.
