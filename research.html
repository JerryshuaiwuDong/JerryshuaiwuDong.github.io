Trustworthy Multimodal AI · PRAI 2026

RadTRACE

When a medical vision-language model fails under coordinated image and text manipulation, can we identify which evidence-processing stage actually broke instead of reporting only a final score?

Clean / Text / Image / Hybrid → Clinical induction audit → Radiology evidence probe → Safety / utility routing

Problem

Safety evaluations often collapse a complicated failure into accuracy, refusal rate, or attack success. That makes it hard to tell whether the model was manipulated by language, lost visual grounding, violated a schema, or simply refused too aggressively.

Approach

RadTRACE tracks behavior across clean, text, image, and hybrid inputs and separates the evaluation into an induction audit, an evidence probe, and a safety-utility routing stage. It also compares prompt-only structured output with grammar-constrained decoding to distinguish formatting failures from deeper grounding problems.

Finding

In the documented experiments, grammar-constrained decoding substantially improved schema-valid evidence and reduced fallback behavior, while negative controls still showed that structurally valid evidence is not the same as genuine image grounding. The project therefore treats reliability as a chain of inspectable failure modes.

My Role

First author. I led the research framing, framework design, experiment implementation, result verification, paper writing, and camera-ready preparation described in the project record.

Personalized VLA · Autonomous Driving

Drive My Way

Can a driving policy combine long-term user tendencies with an immediate natural-language request and express both through closed-loop vehicle behavior?

Structured profile Driving history User embedding Current instruction → Personalized VLA policy → Closed-loop driving

Problem

Real drivers differ in speed preference, caution, comfort, and interaction style. A useful personalized system must combine these longer-term tendencies with short-term instructions rather than reducing personalization to text generation or coarse style labels.

Research Setting

The work uses structured user context, driving behavior, natural-language instructions, CARLA-based simulation, Bench2Drive-style closed-loop evaluation, and a personalized driving dataset to study action-level preference alignment.

What I Learned

The project shifted my focus from static multimodal understanding toward systems that maintain user context and make decisions over time. It also created the foundation for my current interest in memory, preference drift, and safe personalization for embodied agents.

My Role

I participated in the research and became familiar with the data, code structure, and closed-loop experimental environment. This page intentionally does not claim module-level ownership that has not been independently verified.

Medical Vision · Research Foundation

Medical Image Adaptation

How does medical image segmentation degrade when the deployment distribution differs from the source training domain?

Source hospital / scanner → U-Net / CNN / Swin variants → Domain adaptation → Target distribution

Work

I reproduced and compared medical segmentation approaches, prepared source and target splits, worked with training and alignment objectives, and debugged experiments across CNN, U-Net, and Swin-Transformer-style models.

Lesson

This experience made deployment distribution the object of study rather than treating source-domain benchmark performance as sufficient evidence. That perspective directly motivated my later focus on reliability and failure diagnosis.

My Role

I contributed to literature review, preprocessing, model training, experiment analysis, and research engineering. Dataset-specific performance numbers are omitted because the current source record does not verify them precisely enough for public presentation.

Personalized Embodied Agents

I am interested in long-term and short-term memory for user preferences, preference drift, conflict resolution, forgetting, and safety constraints that prevent an agent from blindly accommodating risky behavior.
